# Enterprise AI & Machine Learning Engineering

Ohmtech Developers designs, trains, and deploys high-reliability AI automation systems built for strict corporate security and compliance standards.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Autonomous AI Agents
Deploy multi-agent workflows capable of analyzing complex multi-step data pipelines, executing automated tasks, and generating validated structured reports.

### 2. Custom RAG Architecture
Connect your proprietary internal documentation, CRM records, and technical specs securely with state-of-the-art vector embedding models without exposing sensitive business data to public LLMs.

### 3. On-Premise & Private Cloud LLM Hosting
We engineer containerized AI models using open-weights architectures (Llama, Mistral) hosted inside your AWS/Azure VPC or on-premise hardware cluster.
